## Working Rules

1. Workdays carry the committed plan.
2. Weekends are optional buffer and are not required for the base schedule.
3. Every user-facing task updates `docs/manual-testing-guide.md`.
4. Every deployment records the Cloudflare deployment URL and smoke-test result.
5. The Next.js rebuild is the only implementation target.
6. No community-growth, 3D, native-app, social-graph, or broad multilingual work enters this window.
